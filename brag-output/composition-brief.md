## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: Futuristic scientific laboratory film — moody obsidian glassmorphism, luminous neon raypaths, and live mathematical precision.
- Interpretation: Crisp, authoritative, and visually stunning. Strong contrast between deep obsidian backgrounds and glowing cyan/neon highlights. Generous readability holds on key claims with fast, fluid scene transitions.
- Hook: Laser beam slices open the darkness, revealing the bold challenge to static textbooks.
- Outro / punchline: "See physics. Build physics." — PHYZO logo with WebGL 3D and Live KaTeX engine badges.
- Avoid:
  - Generic SaaS language
  - Abstract filler visuals or meaningless particles
  - Generic flat corporate cards

- Background: `#121212` (charcoal obsidian) with deep radial gradients `#181818` / `#0D1117`
- Text: `#F0E6D8` (limestone), `#C4B9AD` (limestone muted)
- Accent: `#19E3F5` (phyzo electric cyan), `#DD0200` (racing red), `#7D4047` (vintage rosewood), `#E0F7FA` (ice lens blue)

- Visual references from the project: Floating frosted glass panels (`backdrop-blur`, `border: rgba(240, 230, 216, 0.15)`), glowing 3D vector paths, and KaTeX mathematical typography.

## Storyboard
1. **Scene 1: The Hook — 3.5s (0.0s – 3.5s)**: Laser beam strikes across obsidian space. Bold typographic slam: "DON'T IMAGINE PHYSICS." -> "SEE IT IN REAL-TIME 3D."
2. **Scene 2: Wave Optics & Prism Dispersion — 4.5s (3.5s – 8.0s)**: Collimated white light hits a 60° triangular prism; Cauchy dispersion splits it into vivid spectral rays. Live KaTeX card displays $n(\lambda) = A + B/\lambda^2$ and live refractive indices.
3. **Scene 3: Electromagnetism & Lorentz Deflection — 4.5s (8.0s – 12.5s)**: Magnetic field zone with Rosewood/Cyan vector field lines. Charged test particle curves into a cyclotron spiral with Lorentz force equation card $\vec{F} = q(\vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B})$.
4. **Scene 4: Quantum Horizons & Energy Conservation — 4.5s (12.5s – 17.0s)**: 3D hydrogen probability orbital cloud ($n=2, l=1$) with live Energy HUD showing continuous conservation ($KE + PE = E_{total}$).
5. **Scene 5: Outro & Call to Action — 3.0s (17.0s – 20.0s)**: PHYZO icon and glowing wordmark, tagline "See physics. Build physics.", and open-source GitHub badge.
