# Brag Plan: Phyzo

## What is this app?
Phyzo is an interactive real-time 3D physics laboratory and simulation platform that lets you build, observe, and manipulate classical mechanics, wave optics, electromagnetism, and quantum mechanics with dynamic KaTeX mathematical formula derivation and live conservation telemetry.

## The angle
Textbooks force students and engineers to imagine static 2D diagrams. Phyzo turns physics into an interactive, visually stunning 3D playground where equations solve themselves in real-time as light refracts, particles deflect, and energies conserve.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A dramatic dark obsidian canvas sparks to life with an intense collimated laser beam slicing through 3D space, striking a glass prism that splits into a vibrant rainbow spectrum, accompanied by the bold headline: **"DON'T IMAGINE PHYSICS. BUILD IT."**

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Moment 1: Wave Optics & Chromatic Dispersion (3.0s – 7.5s):** Laser rays strike a 60° triangular prism with Cauchy dispersion ($n(\lambda) = A + B/\lambda^2$), breaking into spectral wavelengths with real-time refraction angle readouts and live KaTeX optics cards.
- **Moment 2: Lorentz Deflection & EM Dynamics (7.5s – 12.0s):** A charged particle accelerates into a crossed $\vec{E} \times \vec{B}$ field zone, tracing a glowing cyan cyclotron trajectory governed by $\vec{F} = q(\vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B})$ while dynamic vector arrows adjust in real-time.
- **Moment 3: Quantum Orbitals & Live Conservation HUD (12.0s – 16.5s):** A 3D hydrogen atom orbital probability cloud ($n=2, l=1$) animates alongside the Energy Conservation HUD ($E_{total} = KE + PE$) proving zero numerical drift.

## Outro / punchline (16.5s – 20.0s)
The glassmorphic laboratory UI locks into focus. "See physics. Build physics." PHYZO logo reveals with ambient cyan bloom and interactive shortcut hints.

## User flow worth showing
1. **Select preset:** Switch seamlessly between Classical Mechanics, Wave Optics, and Electromagnetism.
2. **Interact & Observe:** Real-time ray tracing, cyclotron curving, and kinetic collision momentum transfer.
3. **Analyze live math:** Equations and telemetry variables substitute and evaluate per frame without lag.
